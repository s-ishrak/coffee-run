How to play & what’s new

Move with WASD or arrows, or the joystick. Dash with Space or DASH. Pick up coffee and deliver it to your matching desk. Dash into rivals to spill their cups.

Golden Coffee is worth 3 points. Espresso Rush speeds up dash recharge; Coffee Break brings extra coffee. Cup Lids block one hit; Sneakers temporarily boost speed. Pickups activate automatically.

Play three rounds for the championship. Desks and obstacles rotate each round. Two players must ready up to start.

One person hosts. Everyone else opens the same address.
